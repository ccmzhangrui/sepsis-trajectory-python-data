# Operational deterioration and landmark lead-time analysis

Protocol recorded before extracting event counts or fitting these outcome models.

Primary analysis: the existing corrected MIMIC-III trajectory cohort, restricted to an evaluable original SOFA at ICU hour 24 (at least four observed components). Prediction time is exactly ICU admission +24 h. No mortality outcome enters the primary event definition.

An operational deterioration event requires a recorded new mechanical-ventilation or vasopressor-support episode beginning strictly after hour 24 and at or before hour 60, before effective ICU/hospital exit or death, and persisting for at least one observed hour. Mechanical-ventilation episodes use the supplied ventilation-duration table; they are not relabelled independently adjudicated intubations. Vasopressors are norepinephrine, epinephrine, dopamine, phenylephrine and vasopressin; dobutamine alone is excluded. Overlapping/touching drug intervals are merged to form the any-vasopressor support category. A support category must have been absent throughout (hour 18, hour 24]; ongoing support does not count as new initiation. This operational definition detects new support categories, not dose escalation of existing support.

The support episode qualifies only if at least one evaluable SOFA after hour 24, within +/-6 h of its recorded start and before effective clinical exit, is at least 2 points higher than hour-24 SOFA. Event onset is the support start, not the later SOFA confirmation time. Future observations are used only to define and confirm outcomes, never as predictors. If there is a candidate new-support episode but no evaluable SOFA in its confirmation window and no confirmed event, outcome is indeterminate and the patient is excluded from this analysis. Otherwise outcome is whether a confirmed event occurs in the observed ICU episode by hour 60. ICU discharge or death without a prior confirmed event ends event observation, rather than being incorporated into the event. Report these competing exits and the dependence on measurement availability. This is a new retrospective operational proxy, not an independently adjudicated event or a recovery of the original event algorithm.

Sensitivity definition: identical new-support criteria with SOFA confirmation restricted to the preceding 6 h through the support start (strictly after the prediction landmark). This examines whether the outcome relies on SOFA observations obtained after support initiation; use the same frozen predictions where labels are classifiable and label performance as sensitivity assessment.

Models: age/sex + latest four vital signs; age/sex + vital history summaries; vital history + SOFA summaries/components; same SOFA history + training-fold-only GBTM posterior probabilities from hours 6,12,18,24. Five stratified patient outer folds, three inner folds; Youden thresholds chosen from inner held-out scores. Fixed classifier settings and GBTM specification match the already executed analyses. No model/definition selection based on observed results.

Lead time: support-event onset minus the single prediction time of hour 24, summarized among correctly flagged events. Report all-event timing separately, true-positive counts, sensitivity, PPV, false positives per 100 evaluable patients, and proportions of all events detected at least 6 or 12 h early. This is a one-landmark alert interval, not serial first-threshold-crossing warning time. It cannot be compared as a re-estimate of the published 17.6 h.

Inference: 1,000 outcome-stratified patient resamples of fixed out-of-fold predictions for individual model metrics; 10,000 paired resamples for three AUROC contrasts (posterior vs SOFA history; SOFA history vs vital history; vital history vs latest), bootstrap-normal two-sided P and Holm adjustment. Lead-time median CI uses patient resampling among true positives; class-assignment and model-training uncertainty remain conditional limitations.
