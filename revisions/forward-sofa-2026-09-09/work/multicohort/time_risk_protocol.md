# Time-specific validation for the reviewer concerns

Written before extracting the new endpoint counts or fitting its models. This extension answers the request to avoid predicting a SOFA-defined outcome using SOFA slope and to include early clinical exits.

Population: the complete corrected operational-sepsis source cohort (4,856 unique adults, first MetaVision ICU stay, operational sepsis evidence by 48 h and before effective clinical exit). This is broader than the 3,744 trajectory cohort and is not the original published cohort. No minimum 24-hour stay or three-early-SOFA requirement. At T=2,4,6,8,12,24,48 h include only patients whose sepsis evidence is recorded by T and whose effective ICU/hospital/death exit is strictly after T. Exits occurring before sepsis ascertainment are not part of this source cohort; census the full first-adult-MetaVision pool separately to show them.

SOFA-independent event: first recorded ventilation or any-vasopressor episode starting strictly after recorded sepsis recognition and before effective clinical exit. Merge overlapping/touching intervals within each category. Require no recorded use of that same category during the preceding six hours (or the available interval since ICU admission for starts before hour 6). Retain positive observed duration; no minimum one-hour survival after initiation. No SOFA threshold or slope enters the event definition. Vasopressors: norepinephrine, epinephrine, dopamine, phenylephrine, vasopressin; exclude dobutamine alone. No independent adjudication is asserted. The derived tables do not capture all dose escalation or care-limitation decisions.

At each prediction time, additionally require no earlier qualifying support event. Predict the first event in (T,T+12] h before clinical exit. Already-transferred and already-deceased patients are absent from the risk set and enumerated separately. Death and discharge without a support event are competing observation endings; report each and the remaining complete-window non-events. The binary target is the probability of an observed support event before exit within 12 hours, not latent deterioration after transfer. A secondary death model among all sepsis-recognized patients alive/present at T predicts death at clinical exit within 12 hours; hospital death after ICU exit is outside this short-horizon target.

Predictors: identical age, sex and four physiological variables (HR, RR, temperature, SpO2). Compare latest vitals; vital histories; vital histories plus latest evaluable SOFA; vital histories plus latest SOFA and SOFA slope. The first two contain neither SOFA nor its direct scoring variables. SOFA slope is computed only from evaluable observations available by T. Laboratory release timestamps remain unavailable for SOFA-containing predictors. Raw vital chart/store times are filtered at T before hourly median aggregation, and histories are sorted by hour. Record lengths and missingness are retained.

Validation: one fixed five-fold patient KFold allocation over the full source cohort, seed 20260916, reused across all times and targets. Each time/target uses only its risk set, with three stratified inner folds for Youden thresholds. Median imputation and fixed histogram gradient boosting (150 iterations, rate .05, 15 leaves, min leaf 30, L2 5, no early stopping) are fit within training partitions. No outcome-based hyperparameter search. Save private OOF predictions, folds and thresholds. Class counts must support three inner folds; if not, report that model is not estimable rather than fabricate it.

Statistics: 1,000 outcome-stratified conditional patient bootstraps for model AUROC, AUPRC and Brier intervals. A paired 10,000-bootstrap comparison tests adding SOFA slope to latest-SOFA plus vital histories at each time using bootstrap-normal two-sided P and Holm across seven time-specific comparisons. All statistics condition on fitted OOF predictions and omit model-training uncertainty. Report TP/FP/FN/TN, PPV, sensitivity, specificity and false alerts/100 predictions. No cross-time AUC significance claim because risk sets differ.

Lead intervals: support onset minus T among true-positive 12-hour predictions, with median/IQR and counts. Enumerate events occurring before any eligible evaluated prediction time. These time-specific results are not the original serial 17.6-hour estimator, and their fixed horizon caps intervals at 12 hours. Report endpoint timing and exit counts explicitly.
